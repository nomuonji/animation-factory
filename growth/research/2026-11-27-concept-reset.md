Status: pitch only, not validated, not approved to render/publish. The previous cheap-room secret-base still failed subjective editorial review (54/100, not audience data). It exposed the ending and substituted luxury for inventive action. Do not reuse it as a positive anchor.

## Three different mechanisms
A. 終電を逃した人しか乗れない電車 — emotional rescue / public space transformation. Empty platform after last train; a mysterious familiar train stops, seats transform into private overnight berths, arrival next morning pays off the premise. Fictional design.
B. 玄関に帰りたくない人のためのエレベーター — private escape / vertical spatial comedy. A tired resident presses a blank button between home and ground floor; hidden small refuge deploys from the elevator walls; when another rider presses call, everything returns to its ordinary state. Risk: repeats the secret room motif.
C. 4人が住む6畳で全員が一人暮らしできる家 — zero-sum spatial puzzle. Fixed footprint, each resident's furniture intrudes on another, moving walls/vertical beds reorganize it into four independently closable zones; final overhead view demonstrates tradeoffs. Risk: geometry may be implausible, less emotional story.

## Selected hypothesis A
Viewer's concrete frustration: missing last train, low battery, cold platform, taxi cost, nothing to do until morning. Payoff is not a list of luxury amenities: a familiar inconvenience becomes a personally meaningful rescue. First two seconds: station clock 00:48, last train lights disappear, tired person's empty platform; one unexpected carriage light enters. Opening text '終電を逃した人しか乗れない電車' / small persistent '架空のデザイン'. Show actual station/train geometry, not a thumbnail montage. Keep ending hidden.

Storyboard 38–45s: 0–3 last train disappears, clock 00:48, unexpected headlight. 3–8 protagonist steps into ordinary-looking carriage, no vacant bed apparent. 8–15 a seated passenger presses the armrest; seat folds, adjacent partition slides, luggage goes underneath, berth light switches on. 15–22 camera tracks continuous aisle; repeated modules deploy from a common constrained geometry, aisle remains visible; other tired passengers settle. 22–30 train windows change from dark urban silhouettes to dawn; room lights warm, breakfast shelf unfolds by window. 30–38 alarm/arrival display, berth retracts, door opens onto protagonist's usual local platform. 38–42 wide visual reveal: this wasn't a luxury hotel but a ride home in which the missed hours became sleep. No generic narration or list of amenities. Train interior and transformations are speculative, not engineering/real service claims. Do not copy a specific existing train design/brand. Goal: clear silhouette, consistent car geometry, continuous tracking shot, physical transformation and sound design; actual mechanism may need redesign for feasible production.
