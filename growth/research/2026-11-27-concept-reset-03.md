# Concept reset round 3 — daily-life competitive simulation (2026-11-27)
Previous pitches failed because the premise predicted the ending or relied on familiar jokes. New mechanism: a real choice made before an unfolding, auditable simulation; not a predetermined punchline or another aspirational room.
Public metadata evidence, not full playback: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCGBpv_CqYU wooden circuit 143.4M; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9dBz4JU-Bo elimination marble race 6.82M; https://shorti.ai/en/v/youtube_CAPKP_yD7q4 pop-up ball race 52.6M, posted Oct 28 2026; https://famexniches.online/c/max-marbles color cube short 2.5K and country marbles short 3.2K (both ~6mo); https://trari.com/en/story/thesmilyfam-27903/ same channel above-normal early velocity; https://famexniches.online/c/mikan2d also shows long-form competition potential but not transferable conversion proof. Actual viewing and audience analytics not verified. 
Selected pitch: 「レジの列、あなたならどこに並ぶ？」 A familiar and personally irritating dilemma transformed into an overhead diorama competitive mini-game. Opening shows 4 queues, four matching-color player avatars carrying identical 3-item baskets, distinct readable clues: A one full cart (18 items, regular payment); B three shoppers with one item each, regular payment; C two shoppers with 3 items each, mobile tap payment; D one shopper with 5 items and coins visible. Model (fictional not actual supermarket timing): 0.8s per item, 6s normal payment, 1s tap, 15s coin payment. Each entrant has the same 3 items + 1s tap. A completion 23.8s, B 23.8s, C 10.2s, D 22.4s. C wins even though A/D appear shortest. Show actual events/remaining queue movements with consistent clock. All parameters set before render, no arbitrary last-minute mishap or secretly changing speed. Opening 0–2s show queues, ask viewer pick; 2–5 lock in four choices; 5–20 race in accelerated time, visible scan and payment, C advances; 20–25 winner crosses finish, 2nd/3rd/4th result (A/B tie); brief replay highlights visible clues. No instruction to comment, no claims of real measured checkout times; clearly labelled simulation. 
Alternatives: 「同時に押した4人、どのエレベーターが先に来る？」 visible floors, current direction, existing stops; deterministic route rules. 「飛行機から降りた4人、誰が一番早く空港を出られる？」 seat positions, baggage vs no baggage, transfer routes; needs tighter framing, risk tedious. Concept only, not committed to actual channel identity. 
Review: enough coherence to test as prototype, not final pass; ordinary choice may not be sufficiently novel, visual readability of 4 lanes on phone is key, outcome may be inferable too quickly, first frame needs meaningful distinction without paragraphs, simulated events must be honest and fair. Do not claim predicted views, do not publish pipeline tests. Compare actual prototype with two other concrete pitches before release. No scheduled tasks or publication.
