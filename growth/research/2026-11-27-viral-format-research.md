## Decision
Strongest provisional creative hypothesis: desire-driven spatial transformation, not generic knowledge or copied hole construction. Audience need: autonomy/privacy, escape from crowding, owning an extraordinary space, curiosity about hidden interior, visual satisfaction from solving a seemingly impossible constraint. Opening shows a specific spatial predicament, not a title or abstract narration. Every construction change solves a visible problem and creates the next, final reveal lets the viewer mentally inhabit the solution. Add a legitimate design tradeoff that invites discussion; no fabricated factual claim or engagement bait. Geometry/lighting/camera continuity and visible material changes must outperform generic slides. Original fiction/concept clearly labeled; avoid unsafe practical engineering instructions, use imaginative design, do not claim buildability. Primary concept test: fictional adaptive private space in a shared tiny apartment, or personal quiet refuge inside a familiar public-space constraint. Need actual 3D/design capability and convincing previews before choosing. Candidate B: ordinary frustration visual comedy (original writing, no scraped stories); C: physical transformation/scale experiment with real progressive payoff. Do not hardwire one series before execution evidence; 100% virality cannot be guaranteed.

## Pre-publication and validation
Test materially different concrete visual pitches first, independent references and negative cases. Produce 2–3 first-frame + sequence previews offline in a chat-initiated session; not public smoke tests. Judge: mute-first-frame comprehension, desire strength, visible developments, ending worth waiting for, original visual identity, feasible cost, clear fiction. Reject even a beautiful render if the payoff is weak; normal actual video/audio review remains required. Compare language-light international packaging vs Japanese only after inspecting audience and captions. No channel rename yet. Provisional business milestones: repeated 100K then repeated 1M qualified/public separately; measure same age at 24h/72h/7d plus shown in feed, stayed-to-watch, retention, engaged views, subscriber conversion where authorized, review exposure confounding. Do not equate public 1M with qualified 1M. Validate at least two distinct themes; if weak, revise mechanism rather than mass produce. No arbitrary promises or recurring tasks. Current source evidence is promising but not production-validated and not causal; visual references and actual playback remain next research gaps.
